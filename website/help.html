Frequently asked

Quick answers.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Open Order History, select the booking, and choose reschedule or cancel. The free cancellation window is shown on each order.

Which payment methods are accepted?

You can select online card payment, supported e-wallets, or pay on service when available.

How are providers verified?

Profiles are reviewed for identity, experience, service information, and customer feedback before verification badges are assigned.

Is my payment information secure?

The interface displays secure-transaction notices and is designed for encrypted payment processing in production.
